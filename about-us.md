# About ARC Realty

Birmingham is our hometown. ARC Realty is committed to providing the highest level of personalized real estate service Central Alabama has ever seen.

ARC Realty may be a relative newcomer to Birmingham real estate, but its veteran professionals have a long history with this community. Our leadership and agents are involved in the neighborhoods, churches, schools, and civic organizations that make Central Alabama home — because it is home.

For buyers and sellers, that means real advantages: immediate listing alerts the moment homes hit the market, current market reports for the neighborhoods you care about, and professional home evaluations backed by real local expertise.

Today ARC Realty is Alabama’s largest privately owned real estate brokerage — and still runs on the principle in its name: relationships first.
