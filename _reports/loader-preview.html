What the page shows while the MLS thinks

Greater Alabama MLS answers a search in anywhere from one to twelve seconds. Until now the grid sat empty for all of it, which reads as broken rather than busy. This is the replacement, running at real speed — the skeleton cards, the bar and the copy below are the same code that ships.

Try it

Eight seconds is a normal search. Thirteen is a bad day on the MLS — run that one to see the reassurance line arrive at four seconds and change at ten.

What happens, and when

Nothing here claims a percentage, because the MLS never tells us one. The page says what it is doing and stops talking once it can show the answer.

0.0s
Six skeleton cards appear, sized to the real ones so nothing jumps when results land. A thin brass bar moves under the toolbar, and the counter reads “Searching the MLS…”.
4.0s
A quiet line appears: “Still searching — the MLS is taking a moment.” Early enough to reassure, late enough that a normal search never triggers it.
10.0s
The line changes to name the cause: Greater Alabama MLS can be slow at busy times — this will finish. A visitor who knows why will wait; one staring at nothing leaves.
on arrival
Bar, note and skeletons all clear in the same frame the cards render, and the counter switches to the real result count.
reduced motion
For anyone who has asked their system to limit animation, the shimmer and the travelling bar both stop; the layout and the wording are unchanged.
