Hoover, Alabama

Hoover is one of the areas Homes.com lists for Michelle. The city’s schools, where the city sits, and how to check an address before you buy.

Schools: one city district

Hoover has its own school system, Hoover City Schools, with two high schools, three middle schools, an intermediate school and ten elementary schools. The district does not publish a feeder chart on its zones page, so check each address with its School Locator Map.

Hoover City Schools, as the district lists them

HighHoover High School; Spain Park High School
MiddleBerry Middle School; Bumpus Middle School; Simmons Middle School
IntermediateBrock’s Gap Intermediate School
ElementaryBluff Park, Deer Valley, Green Valley, Greystone, Harriette W Gwin, Riverchase, Rocky Ridge, Shades Mountain and Trace Crossings elementary schools; South Shades Crest School

Source: Hoover City Schools, School Zones, read 19 November 2026. The district’s School Locator Map is for information only; for zoning questions call Student Services at (205) 439-1016.

Where Hoover sits

Hoover is in Jefferson and Shelby counties and was incorporated in 1967 (City of Hoover, read 19 November 2026).

Hoover City Hall is at 100 Municipal Lane, Hoover, AL 35216, (205) 444-7500. The city lists two historical districts: Park Avenue and Shades Crest Road (City of Hoover).

Questions

Hoover, answered

Who should I list my Hoover home with?

There is no official ranking. Homes.com lists 17 of Michelle Creamer's sales in Hoover (no time period shown; read 2 November 2026), and many more in nearby Vestavia Hills and Liberty Park. Compare any agent's record on Zillow or Homes.com before you choose.

Which school district serves Hoover?

Hoover City Schools. Its zones page lists two high schools (Hoover High School and Spain Park High School), three middle schools, Brock's Gap Intermediate School and ten elementary schools.

How do I check a home's school zone in Hoover?

Use the Hoover City Schools School Locator Map, linked from the district's School Zones page, or call Student Services at (205) 439-1016. The district says the map is for information only.

Which counties is Hoover in?

Jefferson and Shelby counties, according to the City of Hoover.

Does Michelle work outside Hoover?

Yes. Homes.com lists her sales in Vestavia Hills, Liberty Park, Leeds, Hoover, Cahaba Heights and Mountain Brook.
