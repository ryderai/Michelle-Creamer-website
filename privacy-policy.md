# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 — draft for broker/legal review before launch.

This website is operated on behalf of Michelle Creamer, ARC Realty. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

## Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site (home value requests, market report subscriptions, contact messages), we collect the information you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, and property address — solely to respond to your request and provide real estate services.

## How we use it

Your information is used to respond to inquiries, provide valuations and market reports you request, and communicate about real estate services. We do not sell your personal information. By submitting your information, you consent to be contacted by email, SMS message, and/or phone call; you may opt out at any time.

## Third parties

Listing data, when live, is provided through licensed MLS/IDX feeds subject to their own terms. This site may use standard analytics tools that collect anonymized usage data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Michelle Creamer at (205) 999-8164 or by mail at 3215 Endeavor Lane Suite 113, Vestavia Hills, AL 35242.

## Listing data disclaimer

Listing data on this site comes from the Internet Data Exchange (IDX) program of Greater Alabama MLS. IDX information is provided exclusively for personal, non-commercial use, and may not be used for any purpose other than to identify prospective properties consumers may be interested in purchasing. Information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed. Listings held by brokerage firms other than ARC Realty are marked with the name of the listing broker.
