# Relocation

Moving to Birmingham — or moving away? ARC’s dedicated relocation team and Michelle’s local expertise make either direction seamless.

ARC Realty’s relocation department is led by a Certified Relocation Professional (CRP) trained to work directly with corporate HR departments and transferees. Whether your company is moving you to Birmingham or across the country, the process is handled end to end.

### What the relocation team provides

- Area orientation and personal community tours

- Comparable market data for both buyers and sellers

- Carefully matched REALTOR® assignments in any market

- Travel assistance and scheduling

- Broker price opinions and inventory home management

As a member of Leading Real Estate Companies of the World and a participant in RELO — the #1 producing real estate network worldwide for referrals and relocation services — ARC connects your move to a trusted network in virtually every city in America and beyond.

### Why land in Liberty Park

For families relocating to Birmingham, Michelle’s home turf is hard to beat: Vestavia Hills City Schools serve the area (this mirror does not restate unsourced school rankings — ask the district), the community is 20 minutes from downtown, and there’s a home for every stage — from The Bray’s townhomes to Old Overton’s gated estates.
