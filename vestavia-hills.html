Schools: one city district

Vestavia Hills has its own school system, Vestavia Hills City Schools. Your elementary school decides your middle school. The district lists one high school, Vestavia Hills High School, which has a separate Freshman Campus.

Compare any agent's record on Zillow or Homes.com before you choose.

Which school district serves Vestavia Hills?

Vestavia Hills City Schools. It runs five elementary schools, two middle schools and Vestavia Hills High School, which has a separate Freshman Campus.

Which middle school will my child attend?

It follows the elementary zone. Vestavia Hills Elementary East, West and Dolly Ridge feed Louis Pizitz Middle School. Vestavia Hills Elementary Cahaba Heights and Liberty Park feed Liberty Park Middle School.

How do I check a home's school zone?

Use the Vestavia Hills School Locator Map on the district's Find Your School page, or call the Vestavia Hills Board of Education at (205) 402-5100. Check before you make an offer, because zones follow the exact address.

Does Michelle work outside Liberty Park?

Yes. Homes.com lists her sales in Vestavia Hills, Liberty Park, Leeds, Hoover, Cahaba Heights and Mountain Brook.
