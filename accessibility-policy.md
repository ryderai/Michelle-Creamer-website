We are committed to making this website usable by all people, including those with disabilities, and aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

This site is built with semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable menus, sufficient color contrast, descriptive alternative text, and support for reduced-motion preferences.

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this website, or notice any content, feature, or functionality that you believe is not fully accessible, please contact Michelle Creamer at (205) 999-8164 or through the contact page . We take feedback seriously and will make reasonable efforts to accommodate your needs and improve the experience.
