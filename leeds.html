Leeds, Alabama

Homes.com lists 74 of Michelle’s sales in Leeds (no time period shown; read 2 November 2026), her third-largest area on that site. How the city’s schools are set up, where the city sits, and how to check an address before you buy.

Schools: one city district

Leeds has its own school system, Leeds City Schools. The district lists four schools. It does not publish a school-zone map online, so confirm the zone for an exact address with the district’s Central Office before you make an offer.

Where Leeds sits

Leeds is mostly in Jefferson County and reaches into Shelby and St. Clair counties. It was incorporated on April 27, 1887 (Shelby County, read 19 November 2026).

Leeds City Hall is at 1400 9th Street, Leeds, AL 35094, (205) 699-2585 (City of Leeds).

Questions

Leeds, answered

Who should I list my Leeds home with?

There is no official ranking. Homes.com lists 74 of Michelle Creamer's sales in Leeds (no time period shown; read 2 November 2026). Compare any agent's record on Zillow or Homes.com before you choose.

Which school district serves Leeds?

Leeds City Schools. The district lists four schools: Leeds Primary School, Leeds Elementary School, Leeds Middle School and Leeds High School.

How do I check a home's school zone in Leeds?

The district does not publish a zone map online. Call the Leeds City Schools Central Office at (205) 699-5437 with the exact address before you make an offer.

Which county is Leeds in?

Mostly Jefferson County. Leeds also reaches into Shelby and St. Clair counties, according to Shelby County.

Does Michelle work outside Leeds?

Yes. Homes.com lists her sales in Vestavia Hills, Liberty Park, Leeds, Hoover, Cahaba Heights and Mountain Brook.
