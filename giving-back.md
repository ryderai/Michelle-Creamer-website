# Giving Back

Playing an active role in the growth of the communities we serve isn’t a slogan at ARC — it’s a founding mission commitment.

ARC Realty was built by Birmingham people, for Birmingham. Our agents and leadership serve in the neighborhoods, churches, schools, and civic organizations across Central Alabama — the same communities where we help families put down roots.

Michelle’s own path to real estate ran through service: before becoming one of Birmingham’s top-producing agents, she spent 20 years with a Birmingham-based non-profit, helping purchase and adapt homes for adults with physical and mental disabilities. That experience still shapes how she practices real estate today — everyone deserves a home that truly fits their life.

Locally, Michelle supports the schools and community organizations of Vestavia Hills and Liberty Park, where she has raised her family for more than 20 years.
