How Michelle sells a home

  1. Price it right. Michelle studies the street, not just the zip code, to set a price that draws offers.
  2. Prepare it. Curb appeal, decluttering, paint and staging, chosen for the best return.
  3. Photograph it. Professional photography used across every print and online piece.
  4. Spread it wide. A page on the ARC site and distribution to more than 750 websites, including Zillow and Realtor.com.
  5. Reach the agents. Emails to Realtors, sales meetings, caravans and agent open houses through ARC.

How many homes has Michelle Creamer sold?

Homes.com shows 306 closed sales in the last five years: 202 as the listing agent and 104 as the buyer's agent. Zillow shows 68 sales in the last 12 months and 607 in total. Both read on 2 November 2026.

Does Michelle only sell homes in Liberty Park?

No. Homes.com lists her sales by area: Vestavia Hills 226, Liberty Park 192, Leeds 74, Hoover 17, Cahaba Heights 16 and Mountain Brook 11. Homes.com shows no time period for these counts, and the area lists can overlap.
